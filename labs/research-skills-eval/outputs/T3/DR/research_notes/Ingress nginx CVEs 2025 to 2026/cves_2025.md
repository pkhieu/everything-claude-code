# Critical/High CVEs in kubernetes/ingress-nginx Disclosed in Calendar Year 2025

Scope: the community controller kubernetes/ingress-nginx only (not F5/NGINX Inc's nginxinc/kubernetes-ingress). Method: pulled the Kubernetes Official CVE Feed (JSON), which is the canonical list of Kubernetes SRC-issued CVEs, and filtered for ingress-nginx. Cross-checked against NVD, CISA KEV, the kubernetes.io blog, the security-announce posts and Artifact Hub (for Helm chart versions). Research date: 2026-11-24.

## Which Critical/High CVEs were disclosed in 2025?

### Takeaway
The official Kubernetes CVE feed lists only one ingress-nginx disclosure event in calendar 2025: the "IngressNightmare" batch of five CVEs, published 23-24 March 2025. One is Critical (CVE-2025-1974, 9.8), three are High (CVE-2025-1097, CVE-2025-1098, CVE-2025-24514, each 8.8) and one is Medium (CVE-2025-24513, 4.8). The feed shows no other ingress-nginx CVE disclosed from April to December 2025. CVE-2025-15566 has a 2025 ID but was disclosed on 6 Feb 2026, so it falls outside the 2025 scope.

### Cited Findings

**Disclosure inventory (from the official feed)**
- The Kubernetes Official CVE Feed lists these ingress-nginx entries with a 2025 publish date. All five are dated 2025-03-23, between 17:38:28Z and 17:38:57Z: CVE-2025-24513 (#131005), CVE-2025-24514 (#131006), CVE-2025-1097 (#131007), CVE-2025-1098 (#131008) and CVE-2025-1974 (#131009). The next ingress-nginx entries in the feed are dated 2026-02-02 (CVE-2026-1580, CVE-2026-24512, CVE-2026-24513, CVE-2026-24514) — [Kubernetes Official CVE Feed JSON](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/issues-security/official-cve-feed/index.json)
- The public announcement went out on Monday 24 March 2025 on the Kubernetes blog, by the Kubernetes SRC. It says v1.12.1 and v1.11.5 "have fixes for all five of these vulnerabilities" — [Kubernetes blog: Ingress-nginx CVE-2025-1974: What You Need to Know](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2025/03/24/ingress-nginx-cve-2025-1974/)
- The mailing-list announcement is titled "[Security Advisory] Multiple vulnerabilities in ingress-nginx" — [kubernetes-security-announce](https://groups.google.com/g/kubernetes-security-announce/c/2qa9DFtN0cQ); [discuss.kubernetes.io mirror](https://discuss.kubernetes.io/t/security-advisory-multiple-vulnerabilities-in-ingress-nginx/31950); [oss-security 2025/03/24/4](https://openwall.com/lists/oss-security/2025/03/24/4)
- NVD shows all five as published 2025-03-25 at about 00:15 UTC (the US-evening of 24 March). The scores come from the CNA (Kubernetes). NVD status is "Deferred", so NVD has not added its own primary score — [NVD CVE-2025-1974](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2025-1974) (NVD API queried directly)
- The batch was nicknamed "IngressNightmare" by its finder, Wiz Research — [Wiz blog](https://www.wiz.io/blog/ingress-nginx-kubernetes-vulnerabilities); [Datadog Security Labs overview](https://securitylabs.datadoghq.com/articles/ingress-nightmare-vulnerabilities-overview-and-remediation/)
- Wiz reported CVE-2025-1974 privately to the Kubernetes security team on 31 Dec 2024 (search-result summary, not verified on the primary page) — [ProjectDiscovery blog](https://projectdiscovery.io/blog/ingressnightmare-unauth-rce-in-ingress-nginx)

- Interim mitigation for CVE-2025-1974 is to disable the Validating Admission Controller. With Helm, set `controller.admissionWebhooks.enabled=false`. For a manual install, delete the `ingress-nginx-admission` ValidatingWebhookConfiguration and remove `--validating-webhook` from the controller args. Re-enable it after upgrading — [Kubernetes blog](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2025/03/24/ingress-nginx-cve-2025-1974/)
- The blog explains the impact: CVE-2025-1974 lets "anything on the Pod network" exploit the config-injection bugs through the admission controller. Combined with the other CVEs, that gives "a good chance of taking over your Kubernetes cluster, with no credentials or administrative access required." The blog also says over 40% of Kubernetes administrators use ingress-nginx — [Kubernetes blog](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2025/03/24/ingress-nginx-cve-2025-1974/)
- Credits: Nir Ohfeld, Ronen Shustin, Sagi Tzadik and Hillai Ben Sasson (Wiz). The fix was made and coordinated by Marco Ebert, James Strong, Tabitha Sable and the Kubernetes SRC — [#131009](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/issues/131009) (text via [official feed](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/issues-security/official-cve-feed/index.json))

The `auth-proxy-set-headers` annotation can inject nginx config, leading to RCE and Secret disclosure. Affected: < v1.12.5 and < v1.13.1. Fixed: v1.12.5, v1.13.1. ### Inferences
- The 2025 inventory is complete with four Critical/High CVEs (one Critical, three High) plus one Medium. All share the same affected range, fixed versions (controller v1.11.5 / v1.12.1) and charts (4.11.5 / 4.12.1), because one coordinated release (PR #13068) patched them together.
- Before upgrading, CVE-2025-24514 was likely already mitigated on v1.12.0 because annotation validation is on by default there. On v1.12.0, the Critical exposure therefore came mainly from CVE-2025-1974 chained with CVE-2025-1097 or CVE-2025-1098.
- Inventories keyed only on the CVE year prefix will wrongly count CVE-2025-15566 as a 2025 disclosure.

### Gaps
- I could not reach GitHub release pages (HTTP 403 in this session), so release-note text and exact tag dates for controller-v1.11.5 and v1.12.1 are not verified directly. Artifact Hub chart timestamps (25 Mar 2025 UTC) are the substitute.
- I did not confirm whether a GitHub Security Advisory (GHSA) exists in the ingress-nginx repo for these CVEs. The project's official channel is the kubernetes/kubernetes issue tracker with the official-cve-feed label.
- The CISA-ADP entries on NVD did not expose a CVSS score through the API. Their SSVC content was not inspected.

## Known exploits in the wild or CISA KEV listing?

### Takeaway
None of the 2025 ingress-nginx CVEs is in the CISA KEV catalog (checked against catalog version 2026.11.23). Public proof-of-concept exploits for CVE-2025-1974 appeared within days of disclosure. Neither the official advisory nor reputable vendors reported confirmed exploitation in the wild.

### Cited Findings
- None of CVE-2025-1974, -1097, -1098, -24514, -24513 or -15566 appears in the CISA KEV JSON catalog (version 2026.11.23, released 2026-11-23). No KEV entry mentions "ingress" at all — [CISA KEV JSON feed](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json)
- NVD records have no `cisaExploitAdd` field for any of the six CVEs — [NVD CVE-2025-1974](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2025-1974)
- The official advisory for CVE-2025-1974 says: "There are no known indicators of compromise that prove this vulnerability has been exploited." — [#131009](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/issues/131009)
- Public PoCs were reported on GitHub in late March 2025 — [GBHackers, 29 Mar 2025](https://gbhackers.com/poc-exploit-released-ingress-nginx-rce); [Cyber Security News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/poc-exploit-released-for-ingress-nginx-remote-code/). VulnCheck tracked it in its initial-access feed dated 2025-03-28 — [VulnCheck](https://docs.vulncheck.com/initial-access/2025-03-28)
- As of 25 Mar 2025, OX Security said "there is no evidence to suggest that CVE-2025-1974 has been actively exploited in the wild" — [OX Security](https://www.ox.security/blog/kubernetes-ingress-nginx-nightmare-cve-2025-1974-patch-now-or-risk-exposure/)
- Detection rules were published by vendors such as Sysdig — [Sysdig](https://sysdig.com/blog/detecting-and-mitigating-ingressnightmare-cve-2025-1974)
- National CERTs issued advisories: [CSA Singapore AL-2025-030](https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2025-030/), [CERT-EU 2025-012](https://cert.europa.eu/publications/security-advisories/2025-012/), [NCSC Ireland](https://ncsc.gov.ie/pdfs/2503250145_Vulns_Kubernetes_Ingress-NGINX.pdf)
- At disclosure, Wiz reported that about 6,500 clusters (roughly 43% of cloud environments) had a vulnerable admission controller exposed to the public internet. This figure comes from my prior knowledge of the Wiz blog and was not re-verified in this session — [Wiz blog](https://www.wiz.io/blog/ingress-nginx-kubernetes-vulnerabilities)

### Inferences
- A public PoC exists but there is no KEV listing and no confirmed exploitation. The risk is "weaponized but not confirmed exploited", as of the latest KEV catalog.

### Gaps
- I did not find authoritative reports (CISA, Kubernetes SRC or a major IR firm) of confirmed in-the-wild exploitation after spring 2025. Absence of evidence is not conclusive.
- The Wiz exposure statistic should be re-verified on the Wiz page before it is used.

## Medium-severity CVEs disclosed alongside (context)

### Takeaway
Only one Medium was part of the 2025 batch: CVE-2025-24513 (4.8). It is relevant as a chaining primitive for the admission-controller attack. No other Medium ingress-nginx CVEs were disclosed in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- *Medium*: CVE-2025-24513. CVSS 4.8, `AV:N/AC:H/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:N/A:L`. Auth secret file path traversal in the Admission Controller, leading to DoS or limited Secret disclosure when chained. Affected and fixed versions are the same as the batch (fixed in v1.11.5 / v1.12.1, charts 4.11.5 / 4.12.1). Mitigation is to disable the Validating Admission Controller — [kubernetes/kubernetes#131005](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/issues/131005); [Official CVE feed](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/issues-security/official-cve-feed/index.json)
- The official feed has no other ingress-nginx entries dated 2025 — [Official CVE feed](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/issues-security/official-cve-feed/index.json)
- For adjacent researchers (2026, out of scope), the feed's later ingress-nginx disclosures are: CVE-2026-1580 (auth-method injection), CVE-2026-24512 (path injection), CVE-2026-24513 (auth-url protection bypass), CVE-2026-24514 (admission controller DoS), all dated 2026-02-02; CVE-2025-15566 (2026-02-06); CVE-2026-3288 (rewrite-target injection, 2026-03-09); CVE-2026-4342 (comment-based injection, 2026-03-19); and an entry listed as "CVE-2026-116472" (mirror-target self-DoS, 2026-11-05; [#143852](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/issues/143852)) — [Official CVE feed](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/issues-security/official-cve-feed/index.json)

### Inferences
- In the 2025 batch, CVE-2025-24513 is the only Medium. Its main significance is as a building block in the IngressNightmare chain, for example to place attacker-controlled files that can be referenced via the admission controller.

### Gaps
- The ID "CVE-2026-116472" looks unusual (six-digit sequence) and may be a typo in the feed. It should be verified before any 2026 report cites it.
- Severity ratings of the 2026 entries were not extracted here because they are out of scope.
